# Advertising and Compliance Rules

## Claims
Do not make medical claims. We never say a product treats, cures, heals or prevents acne, eczema, rosacea or any condition. Cosmetic language only (for example "helps skin feel hydrated").

## Substantiation
Any numeric claim must cite the exact study and sample size, for example "94% of 52 participants reported skin felt more hydrated after 2 weeks (self-assessment)". Never round up or drop the sample size or the "self-assessment" qualifier.

## Testimonials
Customer quotes need written permission and must carry the disclosure "Results vary". Paid or gifted reviews must be labeled #ad or "gifted".

## Guarantees and pricing
Only advertise the 30-day money-back guarantee for first orders. Do not advertise discounts unless a promotion is approved in the promo calendar.

## Sensitive audiences
No targeting or copy that references a person's skin condition or age-related insecurities.
