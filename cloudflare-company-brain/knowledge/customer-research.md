# Customer Research Summary (survey of 214 customers, Q2, sample data)

## Who buys
62% are aged 25-34. 71% describe their skin as sensitive or combination. 58% found Glowkind through short-form video.

## Top purchase barriers
41% worry a new product will irritate their skin. 33% feel overwhelmed by long routines. 19% find prices unclear compared to drugstore brands.

## What customers value
Fragrance-free formulas (mentioned by 67%), simple routines (54%), and honest ingredient explanations (49%).

## Voice of customer
"I just want something that doesn't sting." "Three steps is my limit." "I like that they tell me what's in it."
