# SOP: Creative Briefs and Campaign Launch

## Creative brief format
Every brief contains: Objective, Target audience, Key message (one sentence), Proof points (with sources), Mandatory inclusions, Tone, Deliverables, and Compliance checklist.

## Approval flow
Draft by the growth team, review by brand (1 business day), review by compliance (2 business days), then launch. Claims-heavy ads (any number or testimonial) always need compliance sign-off.

## Testing cadence
Launch each concept with 3 hook variants. Judge after 3 days or 1,000 clicks, whichever is later. Retire a hook if CTR is 30% below the concept average.

## Customer support escalation
Allergic reaction reports go to support lead within 1 hour. Do not give medical advice; recommend the customer stop use and contact a healthcare professional.
