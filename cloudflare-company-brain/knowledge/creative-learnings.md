# Creative Learnings (last 2 quarters, sample data)

## Hooks that worked
Problem-first hooks about routine overload ("Your routine doesn't need 9 steps") beat product-first hooks by 38% CTR. Real-skin close-ups outperformed studio shots by 22% in thumb-stop rate.

## Formats
UGC-style talking-head videos of 20-30 seconds had the lowest CPA. Carousel ads explaining one ingredient each converted best on retargeting audiences.

## What did not work
Discount-led ads reduced average order value by 12% and attracted more returns. Ads using clinical jargon had 25% lower CTR than plain-language versions.

## Open questions
We have no reliable data on performance among customers over age 45, and no tested messaging for men's skincare.
