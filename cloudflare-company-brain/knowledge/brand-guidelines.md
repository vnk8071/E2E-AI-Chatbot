# Glowkind Brand Guidelines

## Voice and tone
Warm, plain-spoken and encouraging, like a knowledgeable friend. Use short sentences. No jargon without a one-line explanation. Never shame the customer about their skin.

## Words to use and avoid
Use: hydrated, comfortable, balanced, gentle, "skin barrier". Avoid: flawless, miracle, anti-aging, "fix", "cure", "perfect skin", and any before/after language implying a guaranteed result.

## Visual identity
Soft sage green (#A8BFA3) and warm cream (#F6F1E7) palette. Photography uses natural light and real skin texture; retouching of texture is not allowed.

## Messaging pillars
1. Simple routines of three steps or fewer. 2. Ingredients explained honestly. 3. Skin comfort first, results second.
